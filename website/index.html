Knives, kill effects,and a whole new look.

Knives

New

Thirteen knives held in your own arm, each in its own finish: an engraved butterfly knife, a karambit held in reverse grip, a katana, a scythe, Prism's glowing crystals and more. Each has its own draw, inspect and slashes, and shows in your hand in third person.

Kill Effects

New

Six effects where a player you fought falls: Heaven lifts them into a beam of light, or a nebula burst, a soul rising, starfall, thunder or shattered amethyst. Hit Glow lights up what you hit in your colour.

3D Skins

New

Be a whole 3D character: the Grim Reaper with his scythe, Timber the log with a bat, the Void Walker or an Astronaut, every limb moving with you. Add a halo, a crown, horns or a witch hat, and angel, demon or energy wings.

Weather and the world

New

Weather Changer makes it rain, storm or snow on your screen only, with Ripple's rings on the ground. Colour Grading gives the whole picture a look, and Jump Circles, Block Outline and Death Disintegrate add the rest.

Skies

New

Ten living skies drawn per pixel: Galaxy, Aether, Nocturne, Amethyst, Opaline, Rain, Snow, Fire, Hollow and Afterglow. Set the speed, brightness, tint, stars and time of day, with light rain, snow or embers to match.

Hand Aura and badge

New

A soft glow round your arm in first person, empty-handed or with a knife. And every Eventide player now carries the Eventide logo by their name tag, so you can spot each other on any server.

Four keysto remember.

  • Right Shift

    Orbit and mod menu

    Spin to a tab and open it. Esc in the menu takes you back to the Orbit.

  • C

    Zoom

    Hold to zoom in. Scroll while you hold it to zoom further or back out.

  • Left Alt

    Freelook

    Hold to look around while you keep moving the same way.

  • Y

    Inspect

    Turn your knife over in your hand. Every knife has its own inspect.

Press them now to try. You can change them in Options, Controls, Key Binds, under Eventide.

Questions?Short answers.

Is Eventide free?

Yes. The launcher and the mod inside it are free to download and play.

Which Minecraft versions does it run?

Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.1, both on Fabric. The launcher sets up an instance for each with the Eventide mod already in it.

Do I need a Minecraft account?

Yes. Sign in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

Windows says it protected my PC. What do I do?

The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows warns about it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

How do I open the mod menu?

Press Right Shift in game to open the Orbit, then click a tab. In the menu, click a card to switch a mod on or off and right-click it for its settings. You can change the key in Options, Controls, Key Binds.

Can I add other mods?

Yes. Browse Modrinth from the launcher and add mods to an instance. Their buttons take on Eventide's style to match the rest of the game.

What is the Classic installer?

The standard Windows installer, without the Eventide setup screens. Both install the same launcher.
